History worth keeping.

America's 250th birthday calls for books that take the country seriously. That is what Aldridge is built for.

Patriotic in the original sense: love of country
paid in honest attention to it.

Our books cover the founding documents and the people who actually used them, frontier and migration, courts and schools and local government, regional stories, and the lives of ordinary Americans whose names never make it into the index.

We publish for general readers, for students, and for the teacher who slides a book across a desk and says, start here.
